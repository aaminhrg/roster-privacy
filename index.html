Roster@ Privacy Policy

Effective 19 November 2026

Roster@ is a wallet for business cards. This policy explains what information the app handles, where it is kept, and the choices you have. Roster@ is published by Ahmed Ali ("we"). Questions: aaminhrg@gmail.com.

In short

  • You can use Roster@ without an account. Your cards then stay on your phone only.
  • If you sign in, your cards are backed up to our database in the European Union so you can restore them on another phone.
  • We do not show ads, do not track you across apps or websites, do not use analytics tools, and do not sell or rent any information.
  • You can delete your account and everything stored with it from inside the app at any time.

Roster@ does not read your phone's address book, your location or your messages. It can add a card to your contacts, but only through the phone's own "New Contact" screen, which you confirm yourself.

Photos of paper cards

If you scan a paper card, the text is read from the photo on your phone. A picture of the card, and the logo found on it, are kept with the card on your phone so it looks like the card you were given. They are not uploaded and are not part of your backup. If you pick a photo from your library, the app sees only the photo you choose.

Connecting and messages

If you sign in, you can connect with people whose cards you have saved, and message them. A connection is made when you have both saved each other's card, or when one of you sends a request and the other accepts. A request names a card, by its link or email; it reaches that card's owner if and when they set it up as their own card in Roster@. When you send a request, the other person sees your own card and the note you write.

Messages, photos and files you send are stored in our database in the European Union and can be read only by you and the person you are messaging. You can delete messages you sent. You can block anyone, which stops messages both ways, and report a message. A report sends us a copy of that message so we can review it; we may remove accounts that break our rules. Deleting your account deletes your connections, requests and messages.

If you allow notifications, your phone's notification token is stored with your account so we can alert you to new messages and requests. An alert shows the sender's name, not what they wrote. Signing out removes the token from your account.

Error reports

If the app runs into an error, it may send us a short report: what failed and where in the app's code, the app version and the phone's operating system. Email addresses, phone numbers and links are removed from the report before it is sent. It carries no account, device identifier or card details, so it cannot be linked to you. We use these reports only to fix problems.

Sharing a card as a link

A card made in the app can be shared as a link. The card's details travel inside the link, after the "#" sign, which browsers do not send to any server. The page that opens the link shows the card only in the reader's browser and does not store or send it anywhere.

Photos

A photo you add to your own card stays on your phone. It is included when you share your card as a contact file, and is not part of your backup. A photo you add to someone else's card in your wallet also stays on your phone only.

Organization logos

To show official logos, the app downloads a public list of organizations, such as regulators, banks and listed companies, with their logos, from our server. It matches the cards in your wallet against that list on your phone; your cards are not sent to us for this.

Reminders

If you ask to be reminded to follow up with someone, the reminder is scheduled on your phone as a notification. It is not sent to us and is not part of your backup.

Camera and NFC

The camera is used only to read QR codes while the scanner is open. NFC is used only to read the link stored on a card you hold to your phone and, on Android, to offer your own card's link to another phone while the share screen is open. No pictures or NFC data are recorded beyond the card link itself.

When the app contacts other services

  • The card's own website. When you add a card from a link, your phone requests that page directly from the website that hosts the card (for example, a company's staff-card site), and loads the card photo from the address that page provides. That website receives the request the same way it would if you opened the link in a browser, including your IP address. Its own privacy policy applies.
  • Sign in with Apple or Google. If you sign in, Apple or Google confirms your identity to us. We never see your password. Their privacy policies apply to their part of the process.
  • Supabase. Our database and sign-in system are hosted by Supabase in Frankfurt, Germany. Supabase processes the data on our behalf.

How we use information

We use the information above only to provide the app: to show, organise, search and share your cards, to sign you in, and to back up and restore your cards across your devices. We do not use it for advertising or profiling, and we do not share it with anyone other than the service providers named above, unless the law requires us to.

Information about other people

Cards you save contain the business contact details of other people. You are responsible for saving and sharing those details only in ways those people would reasonably expect from having given you their card. If your details are stored in Roster@ by someone else and you want them removed, contact us and we will help.

Keeping and deleting information

  • Cards on your phone remain until you delete them or remove the app.
  • Backed-up cards and account details remain until you delete them or delete your account.
  • To delete your account, open Roster@, go to Account, and choose Delete account. This permanently removes your account, your saved cards and your own cards from our database. Shared company records, which contain no personal information about you, are kept. Copies in routine backups are removed as those backups expire.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit. In our database, each user's cards are protected by access rules so that only that signed-in user can read or change them. No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete your personal information, or to object to how it is used. You can do most of this in the app. For anything else, contact us at the address above and we will respond within 30 days.

Children

Roster@ is a tool for working professionals and is not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top, and for significant changes we will tell you in the app.
