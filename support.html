Roster@ Support

Roster@ is a wallet for business cards. If something is not working or you have a question, email aaminhrg@gmail.com and we will reply as soon as we can.

Common questions

How do I add a card?

Open the Scan tab. Hold an NFC business card to the top of your phone, scan a QR code, or paste a card link. You can also send a card link to Roster@ from the share menu of another app.

The card did not scan. What can I try?

Hold the card flat against the top back of your iPhone for a second or two, and remove thick cases. If NFC still does not respond, scan the card's QR code or paste its link instead.

Do I need an account?

No. Without an account your cards stay on your phone. Signing in with Apple or Google backs them up so you can restore them on another phone.

How do I delete my account?

Open Roster@, tap the account row at the top of the Wallet tab, and choose Delete account. This permanently removes your account and the cards backed up with it.

How do I share my own card?

Add your card and mark it as yours, then open the My Cards tab to show its QR code or send its link.
